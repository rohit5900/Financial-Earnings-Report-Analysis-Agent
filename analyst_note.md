# Northstar Cloud Systems — Q2 FY2026 Analyst Note [S01] [S02]

## Revenue
Revenue was $2.40 billion, up 12% year over year; the prior-quarter figure was $2.22 billion. [S03]

## Risk
Risk: Management cited delayed enterprise deployments and higher data-center power costs as near-term headwinds. Risk: Two large customer renewals remain under negotiation, creating uncertainty in the second half. [S07] [S08]

## Outlook
Guidance: Q3 FY2026 revenue expected to be $2.45 billion to $2.52 billion, with operating margin of 21% to 22%. Outlook: The company expects full-year revenue growth of 10% to 12% and plans to increase infrastructure investment by approximately $150 million. [S06] [S09]

## Quarter-over-quarter
Revenue up (from $2.22 billion to $2.40 billion); Net income up (from $280 million to $310 million); Operating margin up (from 20.8% to 21.5%) [S03] [S04] [S05]

## Disagreements
No material disagreement was returned by the specialist agents.
